Clam Works Optimizer

Paste your Legends of Idleon save and get the fastest order to buy Clam Work upgrades and spend Scurge points before your next worker-class promotion.

Your save

On idleontoolbox.com, open the account menu (top right) and click Copy raw data, then paste it here. A Toolbox JSON export file works too.

Everything runs in your browser. Your save isn't uploaded anywhere.

What you'll get
  • Clamworks: which pearl upgrade to buy next until your worker-class promotion, accounting for its success chance. Switch to Simple for a few grouped shopping lists instead.
  • Scurge Works: where each Scurge point should go, a Self Affliction switch for farming Scurge EXP vs pearls, a leveling forecast, and a recommendation on what to farm.
  • Your real numbers: paste again after a farming session and it measures your actual pearls and clam kills per hour from the difference. Type a few values from the game (max damage, MULTI-SCALPING) and every character is corrected.
